_Anarekli_ is an independent digital journal devoted to philosophical essays and literary analysis.

The journal aims to create a space for attentive reading and intellectual conversation. The pieces published here examine both classic and contemporary works, philosophical ideas, and the relationship between literature and thought.

Every piece is written first in Georgian and translated into English by the author. Readers can choose the original, the translation, or both versions side by side.

At the end of each piece, readers can share their thoughts, questions, and critical remarks with one another.

Please keep the discussion respectful: offensive and promotional comments will be removed.
