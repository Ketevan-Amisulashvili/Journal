Whoever wrote on this page before me,
their words are breathing still,
beneath the ink, beneath my hand,
like earth beneath the winter snow.

I scrape, I erase, I write again,
and yet each line I add
  carries the shadow of the old,
  the way a child carries a father’s face.

And when someone comes to read it,
they will hear two voices at once:
mine, and the voice of one
I have never seen.
